Arrival activity

Find your archetype

Two minutes at each desk. There are no right answers. After each desk, circle on your passport how much energy it gave you.

Desk 1: Spark

Invent a gadget for someone who keeps losing things. Sketch it and list three features.

You need: paper and a pen

Need a nudge?

  • What do people lose most? Keys, phones, PE kit, headphones?
  • Does your gadget find things, stop them being lost, or remind you?
  • Wild ideas are welcome. You can be sensible later.

Desk 2: Make It

Build the tallest tower you can that stands on its own, using three sheets of paper.

You need: three sheets of spare A4 paper. No tape or glue.

The rules

  • Folding and tearing only.
  • It must stand by itself for five seconds.
  • Measure by comparing with other towers at the desk.

Need a nudge?

  • Rolled tubes are stronger than flat sheets.
  • A wide base stops it falling over.
  • If it falls, try again. That is how builders work.

Desk 3: Tidy Up

These eight steps for making toast are messy. Cut them down to four clear steps without losing the meaning.

Tap a step to keep it. Your taps set the order. Tap again to cut it.

Desk 4: Spread It

Write one sentence to make a Year 5 pupil want to use your gadget, then one for a grandparent.

You need: paper and a pen. Tap a card to find out more about each person.

Desk 5: What If

A pretend app, FindMe, shows everyone's location to everyone, all the time. List three things that could go wrong and one fix for each.

You need: paper and a pen. Try before you tap the hints.

  • Who might use it to follow someone?
  • What if someone is somewhere they should not tell people about?
  • What would you want to switch on or off?
