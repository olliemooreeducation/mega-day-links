How do I get my code onto the micro:bit?
  1. Open MakeCode and click New Project.
  2. Build the blocks shown in the level.
  3. Plug in your micro:bit with the USB cable and click Download.
  4. Do the same on your partner's micro:bit (unless the level says the two programs are different).
